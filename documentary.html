After the Uniform.
The story.

// Subject MatterCohort · 03

Five participants. 52 weeks. One camera embedded with the cohort from anonymous selection through the completion ceremony.

This is not a recruitment film. There are no triumphant montages, no orchestral swells, no platitudes about service. The work is hard. The doubt is real. The friendships are earned.

What you see is the system applied, week by week — the selection, the therapy, the biomarkers, the gatherings — and what it does to people who fully commit to it.

In honor.
In memory.

// Tribute GivingFor The People Who Made The Cost Real

Every cohort begins with names. If you’ve lost someone — or you want to honor someone still here — their name can be the one that funds the next operator’s seat.

N°01 // In Memory

Memorial Gift

Funded in the name of someone you’ve lost. We send a tasteful acknowledgement to the family (or a designated recipient) without disclosing the amount, and read the name at the next Annual Gathering.

N°02 // In Honor

Tribute Gift

Funded in the name of someone still in the fight. Acknowledgement card on request. Used often for birthdays, retirements, or operators who’ve passed a milestone worth marking.

N°03 // Memorial Sponsorship

Full Memorial Seat

$15,000 funds one full participant year in the named operator’s memory. Year-end impact report on the operator funded, with family consent. A continuation, not a closing.
