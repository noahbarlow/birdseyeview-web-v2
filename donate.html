// FAQ
Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes. Bird’s Eye View Project is a Texas 501(c)(3) public charity (EIN 47-4019107). All gifts are tax-deductible to the full extent allowed by U.S. law. GivingFuel sends an automatic email receipt at the time of your gift; year-end summaries are emailed each January. If you need a physical receipt, write to finance@birdseyeviewproject.org.

Where does my money actually go?

Ninety cents of every dollar goes directly to partner-led programs — therapy delivered by The Coming Home Project, biomarker work via Transcend (transitioning to UVA), the 52-week War Feather curriculum, and the annual gathering. The remaining ten percent covers compliance, accounting, and infrastructure. The full allocation is laid out section by section above in Where every dollar goes.

Can I sponsor a specific participant?

Yes — at the $15,000 full-sponsorship tier. Your gift is matched to an accepted operator (anonymously on their side, so they aren’t recruited to perform gratitude). You receive a year-end impact report on the operator you funded, with their consent. Reach out to major@birdseyeviewproject.org to start.

Can I cancel or change a recurring gift?

Anytime. Your GivingFuel receipt includes a self-service link to pause, adjust the amount, change card, or cancel outright. No phone call required. If you’d rather have a human walk you through it, write to finance@birdseyeviewproject.org.

How is my donor information protected?

Payment processing is handled by GivingFuel under PCI-DSS standards over SSL. BEVP never stores card numbers. Your name and email are kept private and never sold, swapped, or rented — we hold to the Donor Bill of Rights in full. See our Privacy Policy for the full detail.

Do you give in someone’s honor or memory?

Yes. When you give, add a dedication note in the GivingFuel form — we’ll send a tasteful acknowledgement card (or email, if preferred) to the person or family you designate, without disclosing the gift amount. Memorial gifts can also be routed to a named participant’s sponsorship.

Can I see your 990 or audit?

Annual 990 filings are published once finalized and the year’s books close. To request the most recent 990, an independent audit summary, or our W-9, email finance@birdseyeviewproject.org. We respond within five business days.

What about DAFs, stock, or planned giving?

Donor-advised funds, appreciated securities, IRA qualified charitable distributions, and bequest language are all supported. See Other ways to give above, or write to major@birdseyeviewproject.org for transfer details, brokerage info, or sample bequest language.
