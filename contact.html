Donor Conversation

N°02 // Partnerships

Org & Sponsor Inquiry

You represent a corporate sponsor, foundation, clinical partner, or operator-led community and want to explore working together. We’ll book a 30 minute discovery call with the right person on our side.

  • Response time: within 3 business days
  • Format: 30 min · video or in-person
  • Best for: corporate, foundation, clinical, community

N°03 // Press & Media

Journalists & Producers

Working on a story, a segment, a podcast, or a documentary that touches BEVP, Ryan Parrott, the 7X Project, or the post-service mental-health space. We’ll reply within 24 hours with a contact and press kit.

  • Response time: within 24 hours
  • Format: email reply with media kit + named contact
  • Best for: reporters, producers, filmmakers
